Sample app icons are downloaded from the official Google Play listing metadata, at 96px. Brand assets belong to their respective owners and identify the apps shown in the sample dashboard.

- Instagram: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.instagram.android
- WhatsApp: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.whatsapp
- YouTube: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.youtube
- Spotify: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.spotify.music
- Chrome: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.android.chrome

For connected phones, icons come directly from Android's PackageManager, through the authenticated per-device icon API. No third-party icon requests are made by the viewer's browser.
